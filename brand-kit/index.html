Colour

Two brand tones, plus three neutrals

Navy and Cream aren't approximations — they're the exact hex values pulled straight from the logo file. Cream reads warm on its own, which breaks the "no warm colours" rule on purpose: it's the real, approved brand tone, not a decorative accent. Glacier carries every link, highlight and call-to-action.

Tints, not new colours: Ink Soft (#5B6777) is Navy blended toward Cream and carries secondary and tertiary text. Ink Faint (#7E8792) is for decoration only — measured, it falls to 3.2:1 on Cream and 3.6:1 on white, below the 4.5:1 needed for small text.

Type

Three faces, three jobs

Fugaz One only ships one weight, so it never carries hierarchy alone — Nunito Sans does every job Fugaz One can't: subheads, body copy, buttons. IBM Plex Mono handles labels and anything that should read as data. Arabic is set in Cairo.

The mark

A bear held in negative space

The mark is the polar bear's face straight from the logo file: ears, eyes and snout all cut from one rounded silhouette, so the animal is made of the ground it sits on. With no accent in the mark itself, it only has to prove itself on light and dark — Navy on Cream for everyday use, Cream on Navy for booth signage and anywhere the brand needs to hold a dark room.
