Why planners book PolaReady

Not a rental with your logo stuck on it.

Most photobooths are the same box at every event. We rebuild ours around yours — then run it on the one thing that has to work every single time: the moment a guest taps the screen.

Designed for your event, not adapted to it

Colours, frame art and layout style are rebuilt to match your brand or theme before the day — a corporate launch and a wedding never look like the same machine.

No guest photo is ever lost

Every shot is saved the instant it's taken — before it's even printed. If anything hiccups mid-event, the photo is already safe; nobody walks away empty-handed.

Flash to print in seconds

The shutter fires a fraction of a second after the tap, and a print is out of the machine moments later — no queue standing around waiting on the hardware.

A full menu, and we'll add to it

Five layouts, ten filters, a full paper palette — all live on screen for guests. Want a layout that only exists for your event? We design it in.

Personal, not generic

Guests can sign their strip or add a message with a tap — small touches that make a printed photo feel like a keepsake instead of a handout.

Everything, after

Once the booth closes, you get a full report — every session, every print — plus every photo from the event, organised and ready to send to your team.
