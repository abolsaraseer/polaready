Why planners book PolaReady

A corporate launch and a wedding never look like the same machine.

Most photobooths are the same box at every event. We rebuild ours around yours — then run it on the one thing that has to work every single time: the moment a guest taps the screen.

Designed for your event, not adapted to it

Colours, frame art and layout style are rebuilt to match your brand or theme before the day — not a generic template with your logo dropped on top.

Built by us, rebuilt for you

The software behind every screen, layout and filter is designed and developed in-house by the PolaReady team — which is exactly why it can look completely different for every event, not just relabelled.

Studio-quality, not selfie-quality

A real camera and a real dye-sublimation printer do the work — sharp, true-to-colour photos, the same quality you'd expect from a proper photo studio.

Everything, after

Once the booth closes, you get a full report — every session, every print — plus every photo from the event, organised and ready to send to your team.
